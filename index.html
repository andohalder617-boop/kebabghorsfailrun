"Where slow-burning charcoal transforms tender spices into culinary poetry."

At কাবাব ঘর (Kabab Ghor), every dish carries centuries of secret recipes passed down through master grill ustads. We infuse authentic Bengali aromatics with classic Mughlai charcoal techniques to deliver a sensory feast that warms both palate and soul.
